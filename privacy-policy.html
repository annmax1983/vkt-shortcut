Privacy Policy

vkt-shortcut · Last updated: September 26, 2026

vkt-shortcut is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not collect your browsing history, page content, or the keys you press. The only time it contacts a server is when you choose to activate or validate a Premium license key.

1. What We Do Not Collect

  • No browsing history, URLs, or page content
  • No keystroke logging — key interception happens locally and is never transmitted or stored anywhere outside your browser
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or cookies
  • No account, email address, or personal profile

2. Data Stored Locally

Everything below stays in chrome.storage.local on your device and is never uploaded:

KeyContents
vks_rulesYour shortcut rules (key, modifiers, website scope, enabled state)
vks_configGlobal on/off switch and Premium status flag
vkt_license_vktshortcutYour license key and its expiry date, if you activated one
vkt_last_validate_vktshortcutTimestamp of the last successful license check
vks_global_device_idA device identifier (see section 3)

3. Network Requests (License Only, Optional)

If — and only if — you enter a license key, the extension sends a request to the developer's license server:

https://api.annmax1983.com/api/license/ (activate · validate · deactivate)

Each request carries only the following:

  • License key — the key you entered.
  • Plugin id — the constant string vktshortcut.
  • Device id — a non-reversible SHA-256 hash derived from general hardware and platform characteristics (platform, CPU core count, approximate device memory, touch-point count). It contains no personal data and cannot be used to identify you.
  • Device metadata — browser name and major version, browser UI language, and time zone. This is used only to show your activated devices in the license dashboard.
Without a license key, the extension makes no network requests at all. The free tier is fully functional offline.

4. Permissions and Why They Are Needed

These are the only permissions the extension requests:

PermissionPurpose
storageSave your rules, settings, and license information locally.
sidePanelDisplay the rule management interface in the browser side panel.
https://api.annmax1983.com/*Allow the license activation request described in section 3. No other host is contacted.
Content script on all sitesShortcut blocking and remapping must be able to run on whatever page you are viewing, so a small script is injected into web pages at document_start. It only reads keyboard events, compares them against your local rules, and blocks or redirects the matching ones. It does not read, modify, or transmit page content.

5. Third-Party Services

None. There are no analytics providers, ad networks, crash reporters, or tracking SDKs in this extension.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. License data is transmitted only to the developer's own license server, and is used solely to verify a license key you provided.

7. Your Control Over Your Data

  • Rules and settings — delete individual rules in the side panel, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.
  • License — click Remove in Settings to delete the stored license key and release the device slot on the server.
  • All local data — uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and reflected in the extension's store listing. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
