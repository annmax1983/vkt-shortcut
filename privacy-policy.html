Privacy Policy

vkt-shortcut respects your privacy. This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Storage

All shortcut rules and settings are stored locally using chrome.storage.local. No data is uploaded to any server.

Permissions

  • activeTab — Used to inject content scripts for key interception on the current tab only.
  • scripting — Required for Manifest V3 content script injection.
  • storage — Stores your rules and settings locally.
  • sidePanel — Provides the rule management UI.

Third-Party Services

No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used. License validation contacts the developer's API only when you activate a license key.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing.
